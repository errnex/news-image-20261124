# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A single 1024×1024 image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026:
**46 real stories across 7 regions**, every story carrying its source badge
(Reuters, AP, BBC, UN News, Kyiv Independent).

The front page leads with Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO after Trump's
intervention (shares +31%) and Ukraine sinking a Russian corvette off Libya,
plus a schematic world map with 13 flashpoints, a UTC timeline of the day,
and region-by-region coverage: North America, Latin America, Europe, the
Middle East, Africa, Asia, and Oceania & World — from COP31 ending with no
deal to Senegal regaining the AFCON title and a 15-year-old smashing 204 off
53 balls.

`main.py` copies the repo-held `entry.png` byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`.
No network at runtime; running it twice writes identical bytes.
